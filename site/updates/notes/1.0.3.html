Captylo 1.0.3

New

  • Captylo AI in meetings. AI notes, transcript corrections and Ask now run on Captylo AI, our AI service in the Pro plan. There is no model to pick: the meetings AI model setting is gone, and History simply shows "Captylo AI" next to texts from Pro.
  • When the meetings AI has no access, an Upgrade to Pro button appears under the message.

Changes

  • Your own AI key works as before for dictation and AI modes, also in Pro. Meetings AI always uses Captylo AI.
