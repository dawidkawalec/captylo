Captylo 1.0.17

One Captylo at a time

  • No more double Captylo. Starting Captylo while it already runs now brings the running copy forward instead of opening a second one. Before, a second copy could start when the app was launched from a script or a terminal, and you got two menu bar icons and every dictation twice.
  • **--help and --version.** Running the app from a terminal with --help or --version prints a short help or the version and quits, without opening the app. --mcp for your AI assistant works as before.

Fixes

  • Changing the app language or granting Accessibility still restarts Captylo cleanly.
