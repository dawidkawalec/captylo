Captylo 1.0.16

Notes everywhere

  • Ask across your meetings and notes. "Ask all" (Pro) now also reads your notes. Answers cite them as N1, N2, and a click on a citation or a source opens the note.
  • Notes for your AI assistant. The MCP server (Settings, off by default) gets two new tools, list_notes and get_note, and search_meetings also lists the notes it finds. It stays read-only and works offline.
  • Short titles. A note without a title shows its first sentence, cut to a few words, instead of the whole paragraph. With AI on, Captylo gives a new or changed note a short title of its own. A title you type always wins.

Fixes

  • A note left empty after New note disappears when you leave it.
  • If a voice note cannot be transcribed, the message says the recording is kept in Notes and offers Open.
  • ⌃⌥⌘N while a recording is still being transcribed tells you to wait; the menu item reads Stop recording while something records.
  • What you dictate into a note is never lost, even if you switch to another note before the text arrives.
  • Open on an older note selects it in the list.
  • The import status in Notes can be hidden and clears when you leave Notes.
  • Meetings says "No meetings" when only notes match your search.
  • Recordings of notes that were never saved are cleaned up at launch.
