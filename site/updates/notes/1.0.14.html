Captylo 1.0.14

Changes

  • Fixes made right before Enter are learned. In chat boxes and in Claude Code you often fix a word and press Enter at once, and the box clears before Captylo could read the fix. While you edit a dictated text, Captylo now reads the field ten times a second, so the last version before Enter counts.
  • Short dictations are learned too. When you dictate one to three words and retype them as a whole ("supa bejs" → "Supabase"), Captylo treats it as one fix of a misheard word. Until now such a change looked like a rewrite and was skipped.
  • Fix a word with your voice. Select one to three words of a text you dictated in the last two minutes and dictate them again. The new take replaces the selection, and Captylo remembers the pair, just like a fix typed by hand. You will see "Remembered: … → …" with Undo, and every decision is listed in Dictionary > Recently noticed.
