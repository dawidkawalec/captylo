Captylo 1.0.15

New: Notes

Captylo now has a third section next to Meetings and History: Notes. Everything stays on your Mac and works in the free plan.

  • Write, dictate or record. Create a note with New note and type, or press Dictate to speak more of it. Your usual dictation shortcut also works inside a note.
  • A voice note from any app: ⌃⌥⌘N. Press it anywhere, say what you want to remember, press it again. Nothing is pasted: the recording and its transcript go to Notes, and the "Note saved" message opens it. You can turn the shortcut off in Settings, and the menu bar has New voice note.
  • Import recordings. Import recording (or drag audio files onto Notes) turns recordings and voice messages from messengers into notes with their transcript. If a recording cannot be transcribed, the note keeps it and offers Try again.
  • Tidy up with AI. Run a note through any of your AI modes (Cleanup, Organize thoughts, Task list or your own). Restore original brings back your text. In Free it uses your own key, in Pro it runs on Captylo AI.
  • Search. Notes has its own search, and a search in Meetings also lists the notes it finds ("Also in notes").

Under the hood

  • Your data is ready for the coming sync between devices: every change now records when and on which Mac it happened, and deletions are remembered. Nothing leaves your Mac yet.
  • The search index is rebuilt once after the update, so notes can be found.
