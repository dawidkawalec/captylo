Captylo 1.0.0

The first public version of Captylo. The app is signed and checked by Apple, so it opens without warnings, and new versions arrive on their own.

Dictation

  • Hold the shortcut, speak, and the text appears wherever you type.
  • By default everything happens on your Mac, with no internet. On the first launch Captylo downloads the speech model (about 1.6 GB).
  • For the best accuracy you can turn on cloud transcription and AI modes that polish the text: with your own key or with Captylo Pro.
  • Polish and English, with your own dictionary of words and names.

Meetings

  • Meeting notes without a bot: your microphone and your computer's audio, with a live transcript on your Mac. Shortcut ⌃⌥⌘M.
  • Optional calendar: the meeting's title and participants, and a reminder before the call. You always start the recording yourself.
  • Search across all meetings: clicking a result takes you to that moment in the recording.
  • In Pro: more accurate cloud transcription, AI notes with tasks and decisions, speaker labels (macOS 15 or later) and questions about one meeting or all of them.

Captylo Pro

  • Sign in with a code from your e-mail, no password.
  • Cloud and AI without setting up keys. Your own key, if you have one, always comes first.
  • Manage your subscription in Settings > Captylo account.

Updates

  • Captylo checks for new versions once a day. You can turn this off or check right away in Settings > App.
