Captylo 1.0.6

Changes

  • Settings and Models: Basic and Advanced. Both screens have a switch at the top. Basic has what everyone needs, while your own keys, model choice and polishing live in Advanced. History, learning and recording storage stay in Basic.
  • You always know where recordings and text go. Models (Basic) shows whether cloud and AI run through Captylo or through your own key. "Change" takes you to Advanced.
  • Captylo cloud first in Pro. Cloud transcription works like AI: in Pro it goes through Captylo by default, even when you have your own key saved. Pick your own key in Models > Advanced.
  • Simpler onboarding. The first launch no longer asks for keys.
