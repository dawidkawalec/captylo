Captylo 1.0.13

Changes

  • Pick the app language. Settings > Basic > App > App language: Same as macOS, Polski or English. Until now Captylo always followed the language of macOS, so on a Mac set to English it showed English even to Polish speakers. The new language appears after a restart, and the Restart button waits until a recording is finished.
  • Language switch on the welcome screen. The first setup screen has a Polski | English switch, so you can change the language before the setup starts.
  • Links in your language. With Polish selected, the captylo.com link, the coffee page, the thank-you page after buying Pro and the subscription pages open in Polish, and the payment page opens in Polish too.
