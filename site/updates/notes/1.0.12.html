Captylo 1.0.12

Changes

  • Dictation no longer hangs on "Transcribing". When the local model is still being prepared (on the first launch, or after macOS clears its caches), Captylo says so right away and transcribes the recording in the cloud if you have Pro or your own key. Without the cloud it waits for the model for at most a minute and a half, then asks you to try again instead of hanging.
  • Low disk warning. When less than 1 GB is left on the disk, Captylo warns you before recording. A full disk could silently stop recordings and the model preparation.
  • Calmer model optimization. The Models tab shows a single indicator, with the time the model preparation has taken so far next to it.
  • Account and invoices in the browser. Settings > Captylo account has a link to the account page at app.captylo.com: your plan, usage and invoices.
